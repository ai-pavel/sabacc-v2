# Music generation prompts

Original music for the game. Use the prompt text below as the
style/description in the music model (Suno/Udio/etc.). Output files
land in `assets/audio/`.

## Main Menu  → `menu.mp3`

> Slow, ambient jazz-cantina piece. Sparse and atmospheric — a modal
> horn motif over a low sustained bass drone and soft brushed
> percussion. Minor key, mysterious and inviting, like a quiet
> cantina before the crowd arrives. Light pad swells, distant
> vibraphone echoes, no driving drum beat. Loops seamlessly, ~2:30.
> Ends on an unresolved suspended chord so it can transition cleanly
> into the in-game track.

## In-Game  → `cantina.mp3`

> Energetic jazz-cantina piece, swing feel and modal horn lead with
> tight upright bass, crisp brushed snare, subtle vibraphone comping,
> and a light kit underneath for tension. Tempo ~110 BPM, 6/8–4/4
> hybrid groove like the KotOR Iziz Cantina cue. Add a short 4-bar
> solo break midway (muted trumpet over a ii–V–i), then return to
> the head. Loops cleanly, ~3:00. Star Wars cantina flavor: alien,
> smoky, playful, with a hint of danger — fits a tense card game
> where the random generator can swing the round at any moment.
